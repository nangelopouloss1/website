CV

I am a linguist working on syntax, morphosyntax, typology, and fieldwork. I am a Permanent Researcher at CNRS – BCL, Université Côte d’Azur in Nice, France. I received my PhD in Linguistics from UCLA, where my dissertation was Complementizers and Prepositions as Probes: Insights from Greek.

My research focuses on the syntax of clausal complementation, argument structure, clitics and anaphora, locality, agreement, nominalization, and cross-linguistic variation. My work combines theoretical syntax with comparative and typological research, including fieldwork on Tsova Tush, Abkhaz, Cua, Tatar, and Luragooli.
